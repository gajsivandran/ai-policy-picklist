Sample language

Communicating AI use in your course

A guide to help you say clearly what you will allow and what you will restrict, in your syllabus and on individual assignments. Everything here is sample language. Take it as a starting point and edit it heavily so it sounds like you and fits your course.

Saying no AI at all is a perfectly good position, and plenty of courses should take it. But “no AI” on its own leaves students guessing about what that covers. There is no official university guidance yet, so whatever you decide, your students need you to spell it out.

  • Put the syllabus statement wherever your academic integrity policy sits.
