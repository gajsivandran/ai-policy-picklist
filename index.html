AI policy language for your course

Pick one course-level statement for the syllabus, then pick language for individual assignments. Everything you choose collects on the right, ready to copy into your own documents.

Adapted from the AI Assessment Scale (Perkins, Furze, Roe & MacVaugh, 2024) and Bloom’s revised taxonomy.

1

Course-level statement

One statement sets the philosophy for the whole course. A course can combine levels, for example exams at Level 1 and projects at Level 3.

2

Assignment-level language

Find the task, then the cognitive goal that matches your version of it.

Before you pick: is the capacity this assignment builds the target of the assignment, or a prerequisite for something else you are assessing?

Target means restrict AI from that work. Prerequisite means AI may be permitted there so students can spend effort on the target.
